\clearpage
\begin{abstract}
本文研究境内上市ETF机会集合下的多资产风险收益配置，构建以Global RRP为主模型、四种配置规则为对照的实证框架。资产池包含\etfCount{}只ETF，评价区间为\evalStartDate{}至\evalEndDate{}，采用\annualizationDays{}日年化和\txCostBps{} bps单边交易成本。研究以多头、满额投资且无杠杆的预算集合对应境内ETF持有组合，并从工具覆盖和融资条件解释其与桥水全球机构配置框架的区别。

主模型先求解凸风险预算参考，再通过资本跟踪、组合方差和预测收益短缺的凸目标生成最终权重。收益目标取当期可行参考组合的预测收益，协方差使用Ledoit--Wolf收缩估计，均值使用20日半衰期指数加权估计。两项惩罚根据生效日前的历史区间年度更新。本文进一步设置目标项消融、首年参数冻结、事前风险匹配及成本、执行延迟和均值响应速度实验，采用配对区块抽样评价绩效差异。

Global RRP的净年化收益为\globalNetReturn，年化波动为\globalVolatility，夏普为\globalSharpe，最大回撤为\globalMaxDD。相对直接持有风险预算参考的\diagReferenceReturn{}收益和\diagReferenceVol{}波动，第二阶段优化在保持相近收益的同时降低了实现波动。收益短缺项体现了收益维持与交易需求的权衡。风险匹配后的Equal Weight与60/40 Benchmark净年化收益分别为\diagMatchedEWReturn{}和\diagMatchedSBReturn。单边成本提高至20 bps及执行顺延一日时，主模型净年化收益分别为\diagCostTwentyReturn{}和\diagDelayReturn。年度更新与首年参数冻结得到相近结果，配合年度候选的弱识别特征，表明模型的经验价值主要体现于配置结构与实施权衡。上述证据形成了固定资产池和既定历史规格下可复现的探索性结论。

\noindent\textbf{关键词 } 宽松风险平价；全球资产配置；凸优化；ETF；交易成本
\end{abstract}

\renewcommand{\abstractname}{Abstract}
\begin{abstract}
This thesis studies multi-asset allocation within a universe of \etfCount{} China-listed ETFs. Global RRP is evaluated against HRP Benchmark, HERC Benchmark, Equal Weight and 60/40 Benchmark from \evalStartDate{} to \evalEndDate{}, with \annualizationDays-day annualization and a \txCostBps{}-bp one-way transaction cost. Its long-only, fully invested and unlevered budget reflects the chosen ETF opportunity set. Differences in instrument coverage and financing arrangements distinguish this setting from Bridgewater's global institutional framework.

The model constructs a convex risk-budget reference and then optimizes reference tracking, variance and expected-return shortfall. The return target equals the reference portfolio's predicted return. Ledoit--Wolf covariance estimates and exponentially weighted means use strictly prior observations, and penalties update annually from earlier calibration and validation blocks. Objective ablations, frozen initial penalties, ex-ante risk matching, cost and execution scenarios, and mean-estimation sensitivity identify the roles of model components and implementation conditions. Paired block bootstrap intervals quantify uncertainty in Sharpe differences.

Global RRP achieves a net annual return of \globalNetReturn, volatility of \globalVolatility, a Sharpe ratio of \globalSharpe, and a maximum drawdown of \globalMaxDD. The risk-budget reference achieves \diagReferenceReturn{} return and \diagReferenceVol{} volatility, showing that the second stage preserves a similar return while reducing realized risk. Risk-matched Equal Weight and 60/40 Benchmark achieve \diagMatchedEWReturn{} and \diagMatchedSBReturn{} net annual returns. Under a 20-bp cost and a one-day execution delay, the primary model achieves \diagCostTwentyReturn{} and \diagDelayReturn{}, respectively. Annual updating and frozen initial penalties produce similar outcomes, consistent with weak identification of the annual coefficients. The evidence supports an allocation mechanism and implementation trade-off conditional on the fixed universe and historical specification.
\end{abstract}
